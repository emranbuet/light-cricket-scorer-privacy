Privacy Policy for Light Cricket Scorer

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Light Cricket Scorer (package com.androidapp.chowdhury.emran.lightcricketscorer), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information.

What the app does

Light Cricket Scorer is a simple tool for keeping score during a cricket match. Scores are kept only in the device's memory while you use the app.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or usage data. Specifically, the app:

  • requests no device permissions;
  • has no internet access;
  • shows no ads;
  • uses no analytics or crash reporting;
  • has no sign-in or accounts;
  • does not access the Advertising ID;
  • does not save anything to your device. Scores exist only in memory while the app is in use and are gone when the app is closed.

Data sharing

None. Because the app collects no data, no data is shared with anyone.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
