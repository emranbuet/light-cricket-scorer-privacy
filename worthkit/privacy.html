Privacy Policy for WorthKit

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app WorthKit (package com.techfinspecial.worthkit), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information. WorthKit is a personal investing tool for tracking your holdings, daily portfolio changes, and net worth.

No accounts

WorthKit version 1 has no sign-up or sign-in, and it does not offer cloud sync. Your data stays on this device. There is no WorthKit account to create or delete.

Data that stays on your device

The holdings, balances, portfolio history, net-worth accounts, settings, and any Finnhub API key you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

You can export a backup from Settings and import it on another device yourself. "Start fresh" permanently erases the data from this device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Market data

To refresh prices, the app makes network requests as follows. No balances, quantities, or net-worth figures are sent for these lookups. Those services have their own privacy policies.

  • CoinGecko: the app sends the crypto identifiers for the coins you hold, to fetch prices.
  • Finnhub: if you paste your own Finnhub API key in Settings, the app sends the stock and ETF ticker symbols you hold (and, if you use Pro comparison features, benchmark tickers) directly to Finnhub with that key. The key stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • U.S. Treasury: Treasury interest rates are downloaded from the U.S. Treasury's public Fiscal Data API. No personal information is sent.

Purchases

WorthKit Pro is an optional paid upgrade. It is sold as a $29.99 yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for new customers, and as a $59.99 lifetime one-time purchase. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID generated by the app, your purchase receipts, and basic device information (such as platform and app version) so it can unlock Pro. It does not receive your holdings, balances, or net worth. We never see your card or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to the Advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Permissions

  • Internet: to fetch market data and to complete or restore in-app purchases.
  • Billing (Android): to buy and restore WorthKit Pro through Google Play.

Service providers

  • CoinGecko provides crypto prices.
  • Finnhub provides stock and ETF prices when you supply your own API key.
  • U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data provides Treasury interest rates.
  • RevenueCat manages purchase status.
  • Apple and Google process payments for in-app purchases under their own policies.

Children's privacy

WorthKit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
