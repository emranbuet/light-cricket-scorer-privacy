Getting started

There is no account to create. Add your holdings and net-worth accounts on the device. Crypto prices refresh automatically from CoinGecko. For live stock and ETF prices, paste a free API key from finnhub.io in Settings. The key stays on your device.

You can export a backup from Settings and import it on another device, or load sample data to try the app.

Plans

  • Free. Track your portfolio and net worth on this device.
  • Yearly (WorthKit Pro). $29.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new customers. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Lifetime (WorthKit Pro). A $59.99 one-time purchase that unlocks Pro for as long as WorthKit offers the Pro features.

Pro unlocks the Compare tab: your returns versus the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, volatility and max drawdown, current U.S. Treasury rates, T-bill / 7–10 year / 20+ year Treasury performance, and a risk-free hurdle. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. In this version, live stock, ETF, and equity-benchmark prices still use the Finnhub key you enter in Settings.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account. Because there is no WorthKit account or cloud sync in this version, your holdings stay on each device unless you export and import a backup.

Cancel the yearly plan

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in your store account:

  • Apple: App Store subscriptions
  • Google: Google Play subscriptions

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. The 7-day trial converts to a paid yearly subscription unless you cancel before it ends.

Your data

Your holdings, balances, and settings are stored only on your device, and there is no account to delete. To remove everything, use Settings › Start fresh, or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
