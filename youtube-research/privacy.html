Privacy Policy for YouTube Research

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the personal tool YouTube Research, developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles information when you sign in with Google.

What the app does

YouTube Research is a personal YouTube channel analytics tool used to review performance on YouTube channels that the signed-in user owns or manages. It runs on the user's own computer.

Google account access

The app uses Google OAuth to request only these read-only scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly

These scopes are used only to read information about YouTube channels that the signed-in user owns or manages. The app does not upload, edit, or delete any YouTube content or account data.

How information is stored

Data retrieved from Google is processed and stored locally on the user's own computer. It is not uploaded to a remote server operated by this app.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YouTube Research's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
