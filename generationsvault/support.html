Support for Generations Vault

If you need help with the app Generations Vault (package com.techfinspecial.generationsvault), developed by Emran Chowdhury, email emranbuet2002@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How recording works

Pick a guided question, or start without one, then tap the record button and talk. Your draft is saved as you speak and can be recovered if the app closes. When you are happy with the take, save it to My Vault. You can replay, rename, add a written note for the book, share, or delete a story later.

Your first story is free

Generations Vault is free to download, and your first saved recording is free. Discarding a take before you save it does not use up that free story. After you save your first recording, you will need Generations Vault Pro to record more. Deleting a saved story does not unlock another free recording. Playback, sharing, backups, and the keepsake PDF book stay free for everyone.

How to unlock Pro

When you have used your free recording, the app offers Generations Vault Pro:

  • Yearly ($29.99 per year, auto-renewing subscription).
  • Lifetime ($49.99 one-time, does not renew).

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. You can also open Unlock Pro from Settings.

Restore Purchases

If you already bought Pro, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings. Use the same Apple ID or Google Play account you used for the original purchase. This is how you unlock Pro again after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device.

Cancel the yearly subscription

The yearly plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancel in your store account:

  • Apple: App Store subscriptions
  • Google: Google Play subscriptions

The $49.99 lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and does not need to be cancelled. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Export or share the keepsake book

The keepsake PDF book is free. From the app, create the book and use the share sheet to save it, print it, or send it to family. You can also share a single story's audio the same way. In My Vault, use “Back up all stories” to export a backup file of your recordings and notes and keep it somewhere safe, such as your computer or a cloud drive.

Your data stays on this device

There is no account, and your recordings are not uploaded to a server. If you delete the app or lose this device, your stories are deleted with it. Export the keepsake PDF and make a backup before you uninstall the app or change phones.
