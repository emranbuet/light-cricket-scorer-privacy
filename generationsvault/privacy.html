Privacy Policy for Generations Vault

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app Generations Vault (package com.techfinspecial.generationsvault), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information. Generations Vault helps you record family life stories by voice and turn them into a printable keepsake PDF book.

Data stored on your device

Version 1 of Generations Vault is local-only. There is no account and no app backend. The following stay on your device unless you choose to share or back them up:

  • Recordings. The audio of each story you record, along with its title, the question you chose, the date, and any written notes you add for the keepsake book.
  • Family details you enter. An optional family name and storyteller name, plus display settings such as text size and high contrast.
  • A recording counter. A count of how many stories you have saved, used only to decide whether your free first recording has been used.

The app does not transcribe your recordings and does not attach photos to stories. If you delete the app or lose this device, this data is deleted with it, unless you previously shared a file or made a backup.

Permissions

  • Microphone. Used only to record the stories you choose to tell. You can deny the permission; you will not be able to record until you allow it.
  • Audio settings (Android). Used so recording and playback work correctly on your device.

The app does not request access to your photos, camera, or contacts.

When data can leave your device

Your stories leave the device only when you take one of these actions:

  • Share a story or the keepsake book. The system share sheet lets you send a story's audio or the PDF book to another app you choose, such as Messages, Mail, or Files.
  • Back up all stories. You can export a backup file of your recordings and notes and save it wherever you choose, such as your computer or a cloud drive.

We do not upload your recordings, notes, or family details to any server.

Purchases

The app is free to download, and your first saved recording is free. After that, you can unlock Generations Vault Pro with a $29.99 per year auto-renewing subscription or a $49.99 one-time purchase. Both are sold as in-app purchases through the App Store and Google Play, and entitlements are managed with RevenueCat.

If you buy or restore Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts from Apple or Google so your unlock can be verified and restored. Apple and Google process payment. We never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat, Apple, and Google do not receive your recordings or story content.

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and does not renew. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and in Settings. For more detail, see Support.

What we do not do

  • We do not have an account system or an app backend in this version.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.

Children's privacy

Generations Vault is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
