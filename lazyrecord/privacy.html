Privacy Policy for LazyRecord

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app LazyRecord (package com.techfinspecial.lazyrecord), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information. LazyRecord is a vertical (9:16) video recorder with a built-in teleprompter, made for recording short videos for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Data stored on your device

LazyRecord works entirely on your device. There is no account and no app backend. The following stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them:

  • Videos you record. Each finished take is saved as a video file in the app's private storage, with details such as its length, file size, quality, date, and whether you marked it as a favorite.
  • Scripts you write. The teleprompter scripts you type or paste into the app.
  • Settings. Your recording, framing and teleprompter preferences, such as resolution, countdown and scroll speed.

If you delete a take in the app, its file is removed. If you delete the app, everything it stored is deleted with it, except videos you already saved to your photo library or shared.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used only to show the preview and record the videos you choose to make.
  • Microphone. Used only to record sound with your videos.
  • Photo library (save only). Used only when you tap Save to Photos, or turn on Auto-save to Photos, to add your finished video to your photo library (on Android, into a “LazyRecord” album). The app asks only for permission to add videos. It cannot see, read, browse, upload or analyze your existing photos or videos.

You can deny any of these permissions. Without camera and microphone access you will not be able to record, and without permission to add to your photo library you will not be able to save videos to your gallery.

When data can leave your device

Your videos and scripts leave the device only when you take one of these actions:

  • Save to Photos. Copies the video into your device's photo library. If you use a cloud photo service such as iCloud Photos or Google Photos, that service may then back it up under its own terms.
  • Share. The system share sheet sends the video to an app you choose, such as Instagram, TikTok, Messages or Files.

We do not upload your videos, audio, scripts or settings to any server.

What we do not do

  • We do not have an account system or an app backend.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.

Children's privacy

LazyRecord is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that change how data is handled, such as in-app purchases, this policy will be updated at this location with a new effective date.
