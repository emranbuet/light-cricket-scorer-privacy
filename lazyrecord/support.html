How recording works

LazyRecord records vertical 9:16 video with the front camera by default. Tap record to start, pause whenever you need to, and resume when you are ready. Each pause ends a clip, and Finish joins all the clips into one video without losing quality. While paused, you can undo the last clip or discard the whole take.

Using the teleprompter

Write or paste a script in Scripts, then pick it on the recording screen. The script scrolls near the top of the screen, close to the front camera, so your eyes stay near the lens. By default it scrolls only while you are recording and stops when you pause. Tap the script to pause or resume the scroll, and drag it to scrub. You can change scroll speed, text size, panel height and background in Settings, and mirror the text for a beam-splitter teleprompter rig.

Framing helpers

Tap the Grid button to cycle through grid styles (thirds, center, quarters, fine, and a face guide). Turn on the safe-zone overlay in Settings to see which areas the Reels and TikTok buttons and captions cover. Grids and overlays only appear on screen and are never recorded. The ring light option brightens the screen edges to light your face.

Saving and sharing your videos

Every finished take is kept in the Library inside the app. Open a take to play it, save it to Photos (on Android, into a “LazyRecord” album), share it, mark it as a favorite, or delete it. You can also turn on Auto-save to Photos in Settings.

Your videos stay on this device

There is no account, and your videos and scripts are not uploaded to a server. If you delete the app or lose this device, takes that you did not save to Photos or share are deleted with it. Save the takes you want to keep to Photos before uninstalling the app or changing phones.

Troubleshooting

  • The camera or microphone does not work. Allow LazyRecord to use the camera and microphone in your device's Settings, then reopen the app.
  • Save to Photos fails. Allow LazyRecord to add to your photo library in your device's Settings (on iPhone: Settings > LazyRecord > Photos > Add Photos Only).
  • 4K is not available. If your camera can't record at the chosen resolution, the app uses the best one it supports.
