Privacy Policy for YieldHound

Effective date: November 21, 2026

YieldHound ("the app") is published by Emran Chowdhury, Tech Fin Special ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What the app does

YieldHound helps real estate investors find single-family rental homes in the United States that meet a target cash flow or cash-on-cash return. You enter a city or zip code and your investment assumptions, and the app analyzes active listings for that area.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you first open the app, a guest account is created with a random identifier. If you add an email address to subscribe or sign in on another device, we store that email address.
  • Searches and saved deals. We store the locations you search, your assumptions (such as down payment, interest rate and expense estimates), the results of each search, and any deals and notes you save.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, we receive your subscription status, product, and renewal date from Apple, Google or Stripe through our payment provider RevenueCat. We never receive your card number or payment details.
  • Technical data. Our servers keep standard logs (such as request time and error messages) to keep the service running and secure.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To run searches and show and save your results.
  • To enforce the free search limit and unlock Pro features when you subscribe.
  • To send sign-in emails you request.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the app, under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions).
  • RevenueCat (in-app subscription management), along with Apple App Store and Google Play billing.
  • Stripe (web subscriptions).
  • RentCast (property listings and rent estimates). We send the location you search, not your identity.
  • FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (current mortgage rates). No personal information is sent.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, searches and saved deals. Cached property data that is not linked to you may be kept for a short time. If you have a subscription through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it in your store account settings, since deleting your YieldHound account does not cancel store billing. You can also email us to request deletion.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your saved data is restricted to your account by database security rules.

Children's privacy

YieldHound is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
