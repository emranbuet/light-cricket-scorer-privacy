Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?
Sign in with the same email, then open Account and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?
On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?
Open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your data. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Cancel any store subscription separately.

Where does the data come from?
Active listings and rent estimates come from RentCast, which draws on MLS and public-record data. Mortgage rates come from the Freddie Mac weekly survey via FRED. All figures are estimates.
