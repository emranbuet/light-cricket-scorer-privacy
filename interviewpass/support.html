Support for InterviewPass

If you need help with the app InterviewPass (package com.techfinspecial.interviewpass), developed by Emran Chowdhury, email ec@emranchowdhury.com and we will get back to you.

Create an account or sign in

InterviewPass uses an email and password account (passwords must be at least 8 characters). There is no sign-in with Apple or Google at this time.

If you forgot your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. We will email a 6-digit code, valid for 15 minutes, so you can set a new password.

Plans

  • Free. Your first resume optimization is free. You can see the ATS match score and a partial preview of the rewrite.
  • Pro Lifetime ($39.99 one-time). Unlocks the full resume, an ATS-ready PDF, and 15 optimizations every month.
  • Plus ($4.99/month, optional). Raises the allowance to 100 optimizations a month and includes everything in Pro.

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. To restore a purchase on a new device, sign in to the same InterviewPass account and tap Restore purchases on the Account screen or the paywall.

Cancel Plus

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in your store account:

  • Apple: App Store subscriptions
  • Google: Google Play subscriptions

You can also open Manage subscription from the Account screen if you are on Plus. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Delete a resume or your account

Open History to remove an individual resume. To delete your account and all saved resumes, go to Account > Delete account. This is immediate and permanent. Purchases cannot be moved to a new account.

If you cannot open the app, email ec@emranchowdhury.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. This is also the web contact for Google Play's account-deletion requirement.

About the ATS score

The score is an estimate based on keyword coverage, standard sections, measurable impact, and length. It is not a guarantee of interviews or employment, and employer systems differ. Always review the generated resume before you send it. InterviewPass does not invent experience; it only rewords what is already on your resume.
