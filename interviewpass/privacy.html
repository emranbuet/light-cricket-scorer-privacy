Privacy Policy for InterviewPass

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app InterviewPass (package com.techfinspecial.interviewpass), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information. InterviewPass rewrites your resume for a specific job and estimates how well it matches that job.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address and a hashed password. We do not store your password in plain text. Signing in is required to use the app.
  • Content you provide. The resume file you upload (PDF, Word .docx, or text), any job description you paste, any job link you enter, and optional formatting instructions.
  • Generated content. The tailored resume (including contact details copied from your source resume, such as your name, email, phone, location, and links), ATS scores, keyword reports, and related notes, stored so you can open or download them again from History.
  • Purchases. Your plan status (Free, Pro, or Plus) as reported by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Account identifiers. A random user ID for your account, which is also used to associate purchases in RevenueCat.

We do not have an analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising or tracking. After we extract text from an uploaded resume, we do not keep the original file; we store the generated result.

If you paste a job link, our servers fetch that public page to read the job description. We do not keep a separate copy of the full job posting after the resume is generated, other than the job title, company, keywords, and related notes saved with your result.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: to create and secure your account, generate, score, store, and deliver your resumes, send a password-reset code if you ask for one, and manage your purchases. We do not sell your data.

AI processing

Your resume text, the job description, and any formatting instructions are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the tailored resume. If a PDF has little or no readable text (for example a scan), the PDF itself is sent to Anthropic to transcribe it. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf under its commercial API terms, which state that it does not use those inputs and outputs to train its models. You should review every generated resume before you send it; the ATS score is an estimate, not a guarantee of interviews or employment.

Service providers

  • Anthropic — AI processing, as described above.
  • RevenueCat — purchase status and entitlement management. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and purchase information from Apple or Google.
  • Resend — password-reset email. If you request a reset, we send your email address and a one-time code to Resend so the message can be delivered. Codes expire in 15 minutes.
  • Apple and Google — payment processing for in-app purchases, under their policies.
  • Our hosting provider — stores the application and its database.

Your session token is stored on your device in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore (or, if you use a web build, in the browser's local storage).

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and saved resumes until you delete them. You can delete any individual resume from History in the app. You can delete your account and all associated resumes at any time under Account > Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Purchases cannot be transferred to a new account. You can also email us and ask us to delete your account.

Children's privacy

InterviewPass is a job-search tool. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
