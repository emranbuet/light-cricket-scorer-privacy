If you need help with the app SyllabusIQ (package com.techfinspecial.syllabusiq), developed by Emran Chowdhury, email emranbuet2002@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Getting started

There is no account to create. Add a course in one of three ways:

  • Upload a PDF of your syllabus.
  • Scan printed pages with your camera, or choose photos (up to 10 pages).
  • Enter the course manually, or load the sample course to try the simulator.

The first time you use AI import, the app asks for permission to send the syllabus for parsing. After it is read, you can check and edit the weights and dates before saving. Make sure the category weights add up to 100%.

If AI import doesn't work

  • Check your internet connection and try again.
  • Keep PDFs under 18 MB. If needed, export only the pages with the grading policy and schedule.
  • If you see a "too many scans" message, wait a few minutes.
  • Dates written as "Week 4" are left blank on purpose. Add them on the review screen.
  • You can always enter or fix a course by hand.

Grades and the What-If simulator

Grade math is done on your device with fixed formulas, not by AI. It uses the weights and drop-lowest rules you confirm. Check them against your syllabus, because your instructor's official grade is what counts.

Plans

  • Free. One course with the parser, simulator and reminders.
  • Semester Pass. A one-time purchase that unlocks Pro for 4 months and does not renew.
  • Annual Pass. A yearly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel.

Pro includes unlimited courses, the GPA planner, calendar sync, and home-screen widgets on Android. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account.

Cancel the Annual Pass

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in your store account:

  • Apple: App Store subscriptions
  • Google: Google Play subscriptions

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Your data

Your courses and grades are stored only on your device, and there is no account to delete. To remove everything, delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
