Support for Fusionista

If you need help with the app Fusionista (package com.techfinspecial.fusionista), developed by Emran Chowdhury, email emranbuet2002@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Getting started

There is no account to create. Browse the fusion recipes, open one to see its ingredients and steps, then tap Start cooking to follow it step by step with a photo for each step. New recipes are added over time; pull down on the recipe list to refresh.

Plans

  • Free. Browse every recipe and cook one recipe of your choice for free. The first recipe you start cooking becomes your free recipe.
  • Subscription. Unlocks the full instructions and step photos for every recipe, including new ones. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel.

To restore a subscription on a new device, open the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account.

Cancel your subscription

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in your store account:

  • Apple: App Store subscriptions
  • Google: Google Play subscriptions

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Your data

The only thing stored is which recipe you chose as your free recipe, and it stays on your device. There is no account to delete. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Cooking safely

Step photos are freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons chosen to illustrate each step and may not show this exact recipe; the written instructions are the reference. Check ingredients for allergens, cook food to safe temperatures, and take care with heat and knives.
