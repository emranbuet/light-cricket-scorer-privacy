Privacy Policy for Fusionista

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app Fusionista (package com.techfinspecial.fusionista), developed by Emran Chowdhury, handles your information. Fusionista is a cooking app with step-by-step fusion recipes and a photo for each step.

No accounts

Fusionista has no sign-up or sign-in. There is no Fusionista account to create or delete.

Data that stays on your device

The app remembers which recipe you chose as your free recipe. This is stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain or Android encrypted storage. We do not upload it to our servers. On iOS it can remain after you delete the app; on Android it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Recipe downloads

Recipes, ingredient lists, step instructions, and photos are downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), where the recipe files are hosted. These are plain downloads: the app sends no personal information with them. Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address when the files are downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Your first recipe is free. A subscription unlocks every recipe and is sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID generated by the app, your purchase receipts, and basic device information (such as platform and app version) so it can unlock your subscription. We never see your card or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to the Advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download recipes and photos, and to complete or restore in-app purchases.
  • Billing (Android): to buy and restore a subscription through Google Play.

Service providers

  • GitHub hosts the recipe and photo files.
  • Wikimedia Commons is the source of the freely licensed photos. The app does not contact Wikimedia; it opens a photo's Commons page only if you tap the photo credit.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • Apple and Google process payments for in-app purchases under their own policies.

Children's privacy

Fusionista is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
